THE BARAZA EXPERIENCE

More Than A Cup Of Coffee.

Baraza Coffee brings together specialty single-origin roasts, thoughtfully prepared food, and a welcoming space designed for conversations, creativity, and pause.

Located in Phase 2 Johar Town, our coffee house embraces third-wave coffee culture—elevating every espresso shot and pour-over through precision and passion.

OUR JOURNEY

Crafting Lahore's Third-Wave Culture

Founded in Lahore in 2019, Baraza Coffee was created to provide coffee enthusiasts with an authentic specialty coffee sanctuary. By partnering with ethical producers worldwide and mastering roasting and extraction techniques, we serve cups that celebrate true coffee complexity.
